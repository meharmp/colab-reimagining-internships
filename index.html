Reimagining Internships
That Work for Everyone

Making meaningful internships easier to access, support, and create.

Internships can be an important part of a student's college journey — but finding and creating meaningful ones isn't always easy. Students, colleges, and companies face different challenges across the internship journey. We brought them together to understand these challenges and work towards practical solutions.

Sunday, August 9, 2026 · TinkerSpace, Kochi

Explore the Draft Playbooks

The challenge isn't simply a lack of internships.
It's a gap between what is possible and what actually happens.

Students struggle to find genuine opportunities. Colleges navigate processes and coordination. Companies often lack the structure or support to create effective internships. And these groups rarely sit in the same room to work through it together — usually one side decides for the others, with a real power imbalance baked in (a teacher deciding what "counts" as legitimate, a company treating a student as free labor, a student with no way to push back).

We believe making internships work better requires everyone in the ecosystem to play a part — and to build the fix together, not have it handed down.
