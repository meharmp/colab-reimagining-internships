From the Room

Snapshots from Co:lab

The option to skip a semester for a real 6-month internship already exists.
Almost nobody knows how to use it.

Students
Most have never heard they can take a semester for this.
Colleges
Unsure how to structure or approve it.
Companies
No system to host a 6-month intern well — so they default to short, unpaid stints instead.
The room
These groups rarely sit together — one side usually decides for the others.

Fixing this needs everyone in the ecosystem to play a part — built together, not handed down.

The Workshop

Getting Everyone in the Same Room

We put students, faculty, and company reps in one room — as equals — to explain the option, name the obstacles, and start fixing them together.

Result: three draft stakeholder playbooks, built to carry these ideas beyond the room.
